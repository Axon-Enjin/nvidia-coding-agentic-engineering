## 3. Eligibility

**Who can enter:**
- Individuals who are at or above the age of majority where they live
- Teams of eligible individuals. The rules set **no team size limit**.
- Organizations: corporations, nonprofits, LLCs, partnerships and other legal entities

**Excluded:**
- Residents of **Brazil, Quebec, Russia, Crimea, Cuba, Iran, North Korea**, and any country comprehensively sanctioned by U.S. OFAC
- Employees of the promotion entities, plus their immediate family and household members
- Judges and their employers
- Parents, subsidiaries and affiliates of excluded organizations
- Anyone with a real or apparent conflict of interest, at the Sponsor's discretion
- Projects that have received **financial or preferential support from the Sponsor or Administrator**. This includes prior funding, development contracts and commercial licenses.

**The Philippines is eligible.** It is not on the exclusion list.

**Multiple entries:** Allowed. Each submission must be unique and substantially different from the entrant's other submissions.

**Pre-existing work:** The project must be newly created, or significantly updated after Aug 26, 2026. If it existed before the hackathon, you must explain what changed.

**Language:** English, or provide English translations.

## 4. The challenge (hard requirements)

Every submission **must**:
1. Be a **working software application** that runs on **Nebius Token Factory** or **Nebius AI Cloud**.
2. Use **at least one NVIDIA open-source model**, such as NVIDIA Nemotron.
3. Fit **one of the four tracks** and be a genuine attempt at that track's goal. A "superficial rebrand of an unrelated idea" fails Stage One.

### Recommended stack (from the overview and resources pages)
- **Nebius Token Factory**: managed model serving and inference, including Token Factory Sandboxes
- **Nebius AI Cloud**: GPU cloud compute
- **Nebius Serverless Endpoints**: real-time inference
- **Nebius Serverless Jobs**: background and asynchronous workflows
- **NVIDIA Nemotron** models at several tiers: Nano, Super and Ultra
- Other NVIDIA open-source models and tools: **NemoClaw, OpenShell, Hermes Agent, GR00T, Cosmos, Sonic**
- **Tavily API**: optional. It is required only for the Best Use of Tavily prize.

## 6. Submission requirements (checklist)

- [ ] **Working project** built on an NVIDIA open model (Nemotron or another) and served through Nebius Token Factory or AI Cloud
- [ ] **Track selection**
- [ ] **Text description** of what the project does, why it exists and how it works
- [ ] **Working demo URL** or hosted app. Not required for Physical AI.
- [ ] **Demo video** on public YouTube, **3 minutes or shorter**. It must show the project working, **with audio explaining how you used Nebius Token Factory and NVIDIA Nemotron**.
  - Physical AI: include ≥1 minute of real hardware operating
- [ ] **Public code repo** on GitHub, GitLab or Bitbucket:
  - OSI license: **Apache 2.0, MIT or MPL 2.0**
  - **README** with setup and run instructions
  - Documentation of **NVIDIA model usage** and **Nebius Token Factory integration**
- [ ] **Feedback** on Nebius Token Factory, AI Cloud and NVIDIA tools and models. This is mandatory, and it also counts toward the Most Valuable Feedback prize.
- [ ] Explanation of what was updated, if the project existed before the hackathon
- [ ] **City event attendance selection**, if you attended one. This is needed for City Winner eligibility.
- [ ] **Keep it available:** free of charge and unrestricted for testing by the Sponsor, Administrator and judges **until the judging period ends (Dec 15, 2026)**

> Judges are **not required to test** the project. They may judge from the description, images and video alone. **The video carries the submission.**

## 7. Judging

### Stage One: pass/fail
Is the project a genuine, viable attempt at its track's stated goal?

### Stage Two: four equally weighted criteria

| Criterion | What judges look for |
|---|---|
| **Technological Implementation** | How well the project is built, and how effectively it uses Nebius Token Factory or AI Cloud models and NVIDIA Nemotron or other open models |
| **Design** | A complete, coherent product experience, not just a technical proof of concept |
| **Potential Impact** | A credible case for solving a real problem for a real audience |
| **Quality of the Idea** | A creative, non-obvious application that shows genuine understanding of the problem space |

**Tie-break:** Scores are compared criterion by criterion, in the order listed above. Any remaining tie is decided by judge vote.

## 10. IP, licensing and legal terms

- **Ownership:** You keep your IP. The work must be original and solely owned by you. You may use open-source components if you comply with their licenses.
- **License to organizers:** The Sponsor and Devpost get a non-exclusive license to use the submission for judging. They may also use it, along with entrants' names, likenesses, voices and images, in promotion for **3 years** after the hackathon.
- **Third-party SDKs, APIs and data:** You must be authorized to use them under their terms.
- **Disqualification:** For tampering with the entry process, breaking the rules or the law, or unsportsmanlike or inappropriate conduct. Suspected criminal activity may be referred to law enforcement.
- **Disputes:** Individual, final and binding **AAA arbitration**, with no class actions. **New York law** governs.

## 11. Strategic read (inference, not from the source pages)

- **Nemotron plus Token Factory must be visible and narrated in the video.** It is a hard requirement and the first criterion in the tie-break order.
- **"Complete product, not a PoC"** appears in both the Design criterion and the challenge text, so polish the user experience.
- **Tavily stacking:** A track win plus Best Use of Tavily ($3k) is a legal combination under the stacking rule. Adding one real runtime Tavily call is cheap and opens that bonus prize.
- **Feedback is mandatory and pays out** ($100 × 10). Write it with care.
- **Judges' titles point to Token Factory, Serverless and Physical AI.** Using Serverless Endpoints or Jobs and Token Factory Sandboxes probably reads as "effective use."
- See `ORG.md` for how each partner's goals map to what judges likely reward.
